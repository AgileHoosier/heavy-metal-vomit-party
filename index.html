WHAT'S PLAYABLE

  • The story: you were in the bathroom throwing up when Rev. Dewey Dollarson's broadcast turned every guest into the Born-Again Undead.
  • Four buttons: A jumps, B pukes (hold up to lob it), X fires a snot rocket: fast, straight, free, and it slimes enemies so they slow down. Y farts: on the ground it leaves a stink cloud behind you that gasses the Undead and blocks projectiles; in the air it's a fart-propelled double jump.
  • Purge them: puke knocks the sweater vest off and sends them stumbling away. Purge one near a working TV and it gets turned again.
  • TVs: puke them until they go off the air. Each one is a bonus.
  • The Tithe Bite: getting bitten costs a heart and floats your score away as dollar signs.
  • Floor B1, The Basement: Undead Moshers, Pamphlet Pushers, possessed rats, and the Possessed Drummer.
  • Floor 1F, The Party: kitchen, living room and den. New Aerobics Undead (jumping-jack hops) and Air Guitar Undead (power-chord shockwaves), Turbo Cola, holes in the floor, and the Concerned Mom, who throws confiscated records, rolls a TV cart at you and yells GROUNDED.
  • Floor 2F, The Bedrooms: hallway, a sleepover, the bathroom and a metalhead's room. New Bedsheet Ghosts float overhead and swoop at you (lob your puke with ↑), a waterbed launches you sky-high if you hold jump, and the Radical Youth Pastor strums homing music notes, skateboards across the room and trust-falls onto you.
  • Secrets: a VOMITRON record on each floor, plus a record player, a ringing phone and a spirit board worth a puke.
  • Floor select: press ← or → on the title screen to start on 1F or 2F.

HOW IT'S MADE

  • 320×240 canvas with 16-bit era touches: shaded sprites with outlines, parallax layers, and colored light from bulbs, candles and TVs.
  • Every sprite is drawn by code pixel by pixel. No image files.
  • Music is synthesized live: distorted guitar, bass, lead, church organ and drums.
  • Keyboard, gamepad, or touch. High score is saved in this browser.
